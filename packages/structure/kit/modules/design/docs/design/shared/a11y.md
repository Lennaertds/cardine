# Accessibility — shared

> **Role:** What every screen must be able to do, regardless of cinematic extras or product chrome.
> **Not here:** landing page keyboard path ([desktop/a11y.md](../desktop/a11y.md)); phone feedback ([materials-a11y](../iphone/materials-a11y.md)).

## 1. Contrast

| Requirement | Ratio |
|-------------|-------|
| Body / labels | **≥ 4.5:1** (WCAG AA) |
| Large text / claim ≥ 24px | **≥ 3:1** |
| Below 3:1 | Out — muted nav/labels are often the trap |

- Text on `.glass`: `--foreground` on the tint. `--muted` is meta on solid, not on glass.
- A photo that breaks the bar's contrast: a stronger `--card` mix or the scroll edge. No text shadow at random.
- **Never colour alone:** ~8% of people have CVD. A state is a word **and** an icon / shape / texture next to the colour.
- Light and dark are one pair. A border visible in light stays visible in dark.

## 2. Focus

- Focus ring: 2px, colour `--foreground` or `--accent`, offset 2px, contrast ≥ 3:1 against whatever is directly behind it. On glass and on an accent button.
- **`:focus-visible`:** a ring on Tab/keyboard; no ring noise on a pure pointer click. Never `outline: none` without a replacement.
- Every link, button and dialog is in the tab order. Hover is extra. The hint is also in the label, the panel, or the destination after the click.
- Skip link to `#main`.
- A dialog traps focus, closes with Esc, and returns focus to the trigger.
- After a route change, focus moves to the main content.
- The sticky bar does not cover the focused element. `scroll-margin-top` ≥ bar height + 16px.
- DOM order = tab order; CSS reordering may not teleport Tab.

## 3. System preferences

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is the end state. No Lenis, pin, parallax, magnetic or marquee loop. Springs become a short fade or nothing. Glass sits still.

`prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`: `.glass` becomes `--card` at ≥ 92%, blur off, the `--stroke` hairline stays. [glass.md](glass.md).

`prefers-contrast: more`: glass ≥ 96% `--card`, border 1px solid `--foreground` at about 40%. No information that lives only in the highlight or the shadow.

## 4. Tapping and typing

- Primary controls ≥ 44px. Dense chips / compact controls may be 36–40px if the craft file says so. [touch-targets](../iphone/touch-targets.md).
- Input fields on the phone ≥ 16px. Never `user-scalable=no`.
- The WCAG minimum for a pointer target is 24×24. That does not replace the 44px.

**Test:** Tab through a screen without a mouse. Reduced-motion and reduced-transparency each on their own. Body reaches 4.5:1 in light and dark. Focus is not hidden under the bar.
